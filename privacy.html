Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Karpa Racing Editor accesses the YouTube account of its owner only, via Google OAuth, to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails and edit their metadata on the owner's channel;
  • read view statistics and YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's channel.

Data received from Google APIs is stored only on the owner's private server, is used only for the purposes above, and is never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. No data of other users is collected.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: karpauapl@gmail.com
